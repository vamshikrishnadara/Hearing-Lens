## Week 1 reflection: foundation

I began Hearing Lens, my ChiEAC fellowship project, by reviewing the requirements
and building the foundation for an analyzer of public comments and community
survey responses. The early work focused on the input schema, dashboard flow,
and CSV/XLSX upload and column mapping. The goal is to help community groups
understand recurring concerns while keeping uploaded data private.

## Week 2 reflection: theme analysis

My next focus in Hearing Lens was the theme-analysis pipeline: cleaning and
redacting text, grouping related comments, generating labels, and selecting
supporting quotes. Testing highlighted why repeated wording, short comments,
and privacy checks need careful handling. The project now includes repeatable
checks and source-traceable quote selection, with limitations recorded openly.

## Week 3 current update

Hearing Lens now has a local analytical core for themes, sentiment, emotion,
and timelines, tested across fictional hearing comments, CFPB narratives, and
federal public comments. The three-corpus notebook records measured results.
Human validation remains a separate step before reporting agreement rates.
Next comes representation-gap analysis and identifying unanswered questions.
